## About Me
My name is Hillary Bruton, and I am pursuing studies in artificial intelligence and robotics. This portfolio documents my work in AI neuroscience, including labs and assignments that explore how biological neural systems can inform artificial intelligence design.

My interest in this course centers on understanding how the brain processes information and how those principles can inspire more adaptive, efficient, and human-aware AI systems.
